Portable WPS/WordPerfect reader for HomeLib Ru

The bundled command-line readers and their DLLs are unmodified official MSYS2
clang64 builds. They run privately from tools/office, do not install an office
suite and do not change Windows associations. WPS/WPD require 64-bit Windows,
including when HomeLib Ru itself is the 32-bit build.

libwps 0.4.14, libwpd 0.10.3 and librevenge 0.0.6 are distributed under MPL 2.0
(the upstream projects additionally offer LGPL 2.1 or later). Their complete
MSYS2 source packages, including upstream sources, patches and build recipes,
are included in sources/. License texts are in licenses/.

libc++ and libunwind: Apache License 2.0 with LLVM exceptions.
zlib: zlib license. No GCC runtime library is included in this component.

Upstream projects and official binary/source package metadata:
https://sourceforge.net/projects/libwps/
https://libwpd.sourceforge.net/
https://sourceforge.net/p/libwpd/librevenge/
https://libcxx.llvm.org/
https://www.zlib.net/
https://packages.msys2.org/

RUNTIME.json records every package URL, version and SHA-256 and every shipped
member. Only readers, required DLLs, licenses and corresponding source archives
are included; compilers, headers, development libraries and documentation are
not needed for running HomeLib Ru.
